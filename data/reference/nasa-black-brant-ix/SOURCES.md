# NASA Black Brant IX reference evidence

The primary source is NASA Technical Publication 20230006855, *Sounding Rockets User Handbook*, Appendix A.4, pages 139–143. The official NTRS PDF retrieved on 2026-07-20 had SHA-256 `2420152937522d053fcb78813ba944181882f7e5314919d47e692610fbe90495`.

The checked-in text artifact is an exact transcription of the dimensional and performance statements used here. Its hash is recorded in `manifest.json`. Metric conversions use exact SI conversion factors.

The Terrier thrust curve and mass, structural dry masses, fin geometry, constant aerodynamic coefficients, and recovery model are explicitly approximate. They exist to create a coherent deterministic visualization trace. They are not suitable for trajectory prediction or historical-flight reconstruction.

Reference scenario, not a reconstruction of a historical NASA flight.
